City or village: your home ward comes first.

The app supports Bihar’s urban and rural location structure without treating same-numbered wards in different panchayats as the same place.

Urban neighbourhoods

District → Nagar Nigam / Nagar Parishad / Nagar Panchayat → Ward

Browse your ward first, then deliberately expand to the urban local body or district.

Rural communities

District → Block / Prakhand → Gram Panchayat → Ward

Begin locally, then expand to your panchayat, block or district.

The preview includes illustrative locations, not a certified Bihar ward master dataset. Real launch requires verified locations and live backend configuration.

People-to-people. Not an official complaint portal.

Bihar Local - Mohalla Charcha has no government APIs, official ticket queues or routing to departments. Civic posts are community conversations and calls for neighbour-led action.

Keep personal details private

  • Your phone number is not shown to other neighbours in the live design.
  • No GPS/location permission is required; choose your area yourself.
  • Do not post exact home addresses, private phone numbers or sensitive personal details.
  • Phone verification is not proof of residency.

A few questions, before you join.

Is this a Bihar news or breaking-news app?

No. It is a local community app for conversations, concerns, reviews and events. It does not claim professional reporting, verified breaking news or an automatic news feed.

Is it a government app or a way to send official complaints?

No. It is an independent P2P network. No issue is forwarded to an official or government ticket system.

What is available in the current demo?

A Hindi/English welcome flow, illustrative ward-first feeds, publishing, reactions, replies, reviews, events and profile controls. Demo changes stay on the device/browser; no real SMS is sent. Live Supabase/SMS setup and launch checks remain pending.

Does the app automatically track my location?

No GPS permission or background tracking is required. You manually select district, urban/rural branch and home ward. Optional post pins use approximate ward-centre coordinates.
