## 1. Who we are / हम कौन हैं

Bihar Local - Mohalla Charcha ek hyperlocal, peer-to-peer neighbourhood
community app hai (Bihar). Iska sarkar ya kisi prashashanik vibhag se koi
sambandh nahi hai. Reports sirf community moderation ke liye hain.

## 2. What we collect / क्या इकट्ठा करते हैं

- **Guest (anonymous) account**: koi mobile number, email ya OTP nahi. App
  launch par ek be-naam (anonymous) account banta hai jiska sirf ek technical
  ID hota hai.
- **Profile**: aapka diyaa naam, ghar ka ward (district/ULB/block/panchayat/ward
  chayan), aur optional landmark (aapki marzi). Ye sirf aapke apne Profile me
  chhupa rehta hai jab tak aap post nahi karte; post ke saath sirf naam + ward
  dikhata hai.
- **Content**: aapki likhi posts, photos (metadata/EXIF hum jaan-boojh kar nahi
  jama karte), comments, reactions, reports.
- **We do NOT collect**: phone number, email, contacts, GPS precise location
  (post pins lagbhag 100m tak gol hote hain), device IMEI/hardware IDs,
  advertising IDs, payment data.

## 3. How it is used / उपयोग

- Aapke ward ka feed dikhane, apne neighbourhood mein baatcheet chalane ke
  liye. Koi ad network nahi, koi tracking/analytics third-party nahi.

## 4. Storage / स्टोरेज

- Data Supabase (PostgreSQL, India/उपयुक्त region) me store hota hai, photos
  private storage bucket me signed-URL se hi milti hain (पब्लिक लिंक नहीं).

## 5. Deletion / डेटा हटाना

- App ke **Profile → "अकाउंट डिलीट करें"** se aapka naam, ward, sabhi posts,
  comments, reactions, reports aur photos turant aur permanent delete ho jaate
  hain. Isse wapas laaya nahi ja sakta.
- App uninstall karne se par antar hi guest identity device pe khatam ho jaati
  hai (recovery possible nahi).

## 6. Sharing / साझा करना

- Aapka content sirf aapke chosen locality ke registered neighbours ko dikhta
  hai. Hum data bechte nahi, na broker ko dete hain. Kanooni aadesh aaane par
  chhodegane doriety check karke he respond karte hain.

## 7. Children

- 13 se chhote लोगों के liye yeh app nahi hai.

## 8. Changes

- Yeh policy update ho sakti hai; bahut महत्वपूर्ण बदलाव app ke andar dikhaye
  jayenge.
